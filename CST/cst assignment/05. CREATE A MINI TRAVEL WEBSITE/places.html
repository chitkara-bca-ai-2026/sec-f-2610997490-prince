A war memorial in the heart of Delhi built to remember Indian soldiers.

The Palace of Winds, famous for its pink sandstone and hundreds of small windows.

A big stone arch built near the sea, a famous landmark of Mumbai.

The holy Sikh temple covered in gold, surrounded by a peaceful sacred pool.
